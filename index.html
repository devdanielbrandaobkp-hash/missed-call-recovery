What we built

Inside the tools the company already pays for. No new app for the team to learn.

01

Calls ring in order

Day line first, then the evening line, then the owner, about 20 seconds each. After hours, calls go straight to voicemail.

02

Missed caller becomes a lead

A call from a number not in the CRM creates a lead in BuilderPrime with source "Phone", within minutes.

03

Whoever's on the phones is told

A RingCentral message and an email with the caller's number, plus whether they left a voicemail.

04

Callback is tracked, owner backs it up

A real conversation closes it out. Nobody reached them within an hour? The owner gets an alert.

05

Daily scorecard

Every morning: calls by hour and day, answer rate, callback speed, who's still waiting, calls that became appointments.

Runs on what you already have. RingCentral, BuilderPrime and a Google Workspace account. No new software, no new logins for the team.
Your data stays yours. The automation, the alerts and the daily sheet all live in the company's own Google account.
What it takes. RingCentral admin access and a BuilderPrime API key. About a week to set up, starting with the same call-log analysis on your own numbers.
What the owner sees. One sheet that answers "did anyone call them back?" and the numbers for next year's budget, without asking the team.
Data note: Figures come from the company's RingCentral call log (Aug 18 – Oct 12, 2026), matched to BuilderPrime by phone number.
